Questions

Questions people ask.

What is an AEO Card?

A one-card summary of how ready your website is for AI answer engines. It grades four things, whether AI can reach the page, tell what the site does, extract useful answers, and trust it enough to cite it, and names the one change most worth making first.

Does a good score mean ChatGPT will cite me?

No. Nobody can promise that. The score tells you whether your page is easy or hard for AI systems to reach, understand and quote. Fixing what it flags removes obstacles; it doesn't buy placement.

What does the scanner actually read?

Your homepage HTML, response headers, robots.txt, whether a sitemap exists, and whether an llms.txt file exists. It follows up to five redirects and reads at most 1.5 MB. It doesn't run JavaScript, log in, or crawl other pages.

Is it free? Do you store my site?

Free, no login. Results are cached for about ten minutes so repeat checks are fast. Nothing else is stored.
