                What the score means
                
      
                The 0–100 score is a naming-distinctiveness heuristic. It rewards names that avoid crowded startup patterns and don't look or sound like well-known products or the competitors you list. It is not a trademark score, not legal clearance, not a guarantee that the name is unique in search, and not a guarantee that the name can be registered.
                
      
                Working out how to name a micro SaaS? Shortlist a few names, run each one through this social media username checker and domain check, and drop any that are taken on .com or on the platforms your buyers actually use.
                
    
                

    
                
      
                Questions
                
      
                
        
                
          
                Is the idea feedback real validation?
                
          
                No. It's an AI's first read, meant to sharpen a rough idea and point out risks. Real validation is people paying or signing up, which is what the 7-day test is for.
                
        
                
        
                
          
                What happens to the idea I type?
                
          
                It's sent to Google's Gemini API to write your report. NamerCheck doesn't save it, and it isn't added to your recent checks. Google's API terms apply to what Google receives.
                
        
                
        
                
          
                Is NamerCheck telling me the name is legally safe?
                
          
                No. It checks technical signals like registration, visible usernames, and naming similarity. Trademark and legal clearance are separate work.
                
        
                
        
                
          
                Why does a result sometimes say "Likely Available"?
                
          
                Because "not currently found" and "definitely available for you to claim" are not always the same thing. Registries and platforms can reserve names.
                
        
                
        
                
          
                Why can't NamerCheck verify every social network?
                
          
                Some platforms block automated checks, require authenticated APIs, rate-limit requests, or return ambiguous pages. NamerCheck reports uncertainty instead of guessing.
                
        
                
        
                
          
                Do you store the names I check?
                
          
                There is no server-side name database. Recent checks are stored locally in your browser and can be cleared at any time.
