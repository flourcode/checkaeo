Questions

Questions people ask.

What does Aeoden do?

It checks the files and signals AI systems like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity use to read your website, marks each one Ready, Needs fix or Missing, and writes what you need: llms.txt, structured data, AI crawler rules, and an ai-catalog.json if you offer agent tools. Everything is generated from your own site.

Will these files get me cited by ChatGPT?

No file can promise that, and we won't pretend otherwise. Structured data and clear crawler rules are well-established ways to help machines read a site. llms.txt is a widely adopted convention, but no major AI provider has confirmed using it when composing answers. ai-catalog.json is a draft standard (June 2026) for sites that offer agent tools. They're small, cheap to install, and remove obstacles; they don't buy placement.

What is ai-catalog.json, and do I need one?

It's the manifest from the Agentic Resource Discovery (ARD) draft standard, served at /.well-known/ai-catalog.json. It lists the agent resources your domain offers, such as MCP servers, AI agents and skills. If you don't offer any, you don't need one. An empty catalog is valid and simply names you as the publisher. Web pages don't belong in it; list them in llms.txt instead.

Why no score?

Because a number invites arguments and doesn't tell you what to do. A file is either in place or it isn't, and if it isn't, we hand it to you. We still show what we noticed on your page and whether AI already knows you as an entity, as facts rather than a grade.

What does the scanner actually read?

Your homepage HTML, response headers, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt and /.well-known/ai-catalog.json, plus one Wikidata lookup. It follows up to five redirects and reads at most 1.5 MB. It doesn't run JavaScript, log in, or crawl other pages.

Is it free? Do you store my site?

Free, no login. Results are cached for about ten minutes so repeat checks are fast. Nothing else is stored. Your AI files are generated during the scan and downloaded straight to your device.
