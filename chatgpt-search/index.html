Engine note

ChatGPT search readiness

Which crawlers matter, what gets read, and what gets quoted.

Engine noteUpdated / 2026-09-30

ChatGPT search fetches live web pages to answer questions and shows its sources. To appear in those sources, your page has to be reachable by OpenAI's crawler, readable without JavaScript, and clear enough to quote.

Which crawlers OpenAI uses

User agentWhat it doesAffects search answers?
OAI-SearchBotCrawls and indexes pages for ChatGPT search resultsYes
ChatGPT-UserFetches a page on demand when a user's request needs itYes
GPTBotCollects data that may be used to train modelsNo

The distinction matters. A robots.txt that disallows GPTBot opts you out of training and leaves search alone. A robots.txt that disallows OAI-SearchBot removes you from ChatGPT search. Many "block AI" snippets circulating online disallow all three. Check yours. The exact user-agent list is maintained on OpenAI's site; see sources.

What ChatGPT search reads

Publicly, OpenAI has said its search combines its own crawling with partner search indexes (historically Bing). In practice that means the same fundamentals as any search engine, with a heavier tilt toward the raw HTML: crawlers of this kind generally do not execute JavaScript, so a page whose content appears only after scripts run looks empty to them.

What gets quoted

Answers are composed from short passages. The passages that get used are the ones that stand alone: a direct opening sentence, a question heading with a two-sentence answer beneath it, a definition, a short list. Marketing copy that needs the surrounding page to make sense is not quotable and tends to be skipped.

A checklist

  1. Homepage returns 200 over HTTPS with no noindex.
  2. robots.txt does not disallow OAI-SearchBot or ChatGPT-User.
  3. Your H1 and first paragraph are present in the raw HTML (check "view source").
  4. The first paragraph says what you do, in one sentence, with no adjectives you would not say out loud.
  5. At least a few headings are phrased as the questions people actually ask.
  6. An About page and a contact path are linked from every page.

A CheckAEO scan checks all six.

Questions people ask

How do I check whether ChatGPT can see my site?

Run a CheckAEO scan. The Access category reports your HTTP status, noindex directives, and whether OAI-SearchBot is blocked in robots.txt, and shows how much text is in the raw HTML.

Should I block GPTBot?

That is your decision and it concerns model training, not search. Blocking GPTBot does not stop ChatGPT search from fetching your page; blocking OAI-SearchBot does.

Does ChatGPT use my structured data?

There is no official statement. Structured data is still worthwhile because it feeds Bing and Google, which ChatGPT search builds on, and because it states your name and identity unambiguously.
