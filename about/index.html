Purpose

CheckAEO is a small public utility that checks whether answer engines can reach, understand, quote and trust a website, and returns the result as one shareable record: the AEO Card.

Why this exists

Most "AI visibility" tools either overpromise, with guaranteed citations and secret tricks, or overwhelm, with forty metrics in a dashboard nobody reads. CheckAEO does the opposite: one card, four honest questions, and the three things to fix first.

What it is not

It is not a ranking predictor. It does not know whether ChatGPT will cite you, and neither does anyone else. It measures readiness: whether the obstacles are out of the way. That is a smaller claim, and a true one.

Operating notes

A small serverless scanner fetches your homepage the way a crawler does, as raw HTML without JavaScript, along with robots.txt, sitemap and headers, then scores every check we publish. The card is drawn on your device, and exports are generated there too. Sources are listed on the sources page.

Crawler

Scans identify themselves as CheckAEOBot/1.0 (+https://checkaeo.com/about/). They fetch one page plus three small public files, follow at most five redirects, read at most 1.5 MB, and only run when a person asks for a scan. To opt out, disallow CheckAEOBot in your robots.txt.

Questions people ask

Who runs CheckAEO?

CheckAEO is built and run by Mark Flournoy. You can book a call with him or message him on LinkedIn.

Does the scanner store my pages?

No. Results are cached for about ten minutes so repeat scans are quick, and then discarded. We do not store page content.

Can I use my AEO Card in a client report or a LinkedIn post?

Yes. That is what it is for. Download it as a PNG or SVG, or copy the summary text. Please keep the checkaeo.com attribution on the card.
