AEO vs SEO

Almost everything overlaps. Here is exactly where the emphasis shifts.

ComparisonUpdated / 2026-09-30

SEO makes your site easy for search engines to rank. AEO makes it easy for AI answer engines to read, quote and cite. They overlap almost entirely, and where they differ, the difference is emphasis, not contradiction.

Where they are the same

  • Crawlability. Both need a reachable page over HTTPS, a sane robots.txt, no noindex, and a sitemap.
  • Clear titles and headings. Both read the title, description and H1 first.
  • Structured data. Organization, WebSite, Article and FAQPage schema help both.
  • Trust signals. About pages, contact details, authorship and dates matter to both. Google calls it E-E-A-T; answer engines do not have a name for it but behave the same way.

The one mistake that hurts both

Blocking AI crawlers wholesale. Robots.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended control training; rules for OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot and PerplexityBot control live answers. Sites that copy a "block all AI" snippet usually block both, and then wonder why they never appear in ChatGPT search. CheckAEO reports these separately and only scores the ones that affect answers.

A practical order of operations

  1. Fix access: 200 response, HTTPS, no noindex, answer-engine crawlers allowed, content in the HTML.
  2. Fix clarity: title, description and H1 that say what you do and for whom.
  3. Fix answers: answer-first opening, question headings, a short FAQ, self-contained paragraphs.
  4. Fix trust: About, contact, Organization schema, dates and bylines where relevant.

That is also the order CheckAEO weights its score.

Questions people ask

Does AEO replace SEO?

No. AI answer engines still rely on search indexes and crawlers, so a site that is invisible to Google is usually invisible to them too. AEO is a shift in emphasis on top of SEO, not a replacement.

If I only have time for one thing, what should it be?

Make the top of your homepage say, in one plain sentence, what you offer and who it is for. It improves both SEO and AEO, and it is the most common failure we see.

Do keywords still matter?

Less as exact phrases, more as plain language. Answer engines match meaning, so writing the way your customers actually describe their problem works better than repeating a keyword.
