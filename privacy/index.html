Privacy

What is sent, what is kept, and for how long.

PrivacyUpdated / 2026-09-30

Aeoden has no accounts and stores no page content. This page lists exactly what happens when you use it.

When you run a scan

  • The URL you enter is sent to our scanner, which fetches that public page plus its robots.txt, sitemap.xml and llms.txt.
  • The result is cached for about ten minutes so repeat scans are fast, then discarded.
  • The scanner keeps a short-lived, in-memory count of requests per IP address to prevent abuse. It is not written anywhere.

Your files and card

Your AI files are generated during the check and downloaded straight to your device. Your status card, and any image you download, is drawn in your browser. Nothing is uploaded when you export it.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled on this site, Google Analytics 4 records page views and a small set of anonymous events (a scan started or finished, a card downloaded, a contact link clicked) with IP anonymisation. No analytics script loads if it is not configured.

Contact links

"Talk to Mark" opens Calendly and "Message on LinkedIn" opens LinkedIn. Those services have their own privacy policies.
